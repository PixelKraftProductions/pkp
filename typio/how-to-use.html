HOW TO USE TYPiO

From setup to
steady flow.

A clear quick start for your first lesson, practice session and progress review.

01

DEVICE SETUP

Connect a physical keyboard

Pair a Bluetooth keyboard or connect a compatible USB keyboard to your Android device. Check Android’s physical keyboard settings and confirm the keyboard can type in another app.

For typing sessionsTYPiO uses your external keyboard for lessons, practice and tests. The app’s typing area is not an on-screen keyboard.
02

CHOOSE A COURSE

Select a language and layout

In TYPiO, choose English QWERTY, Hindi Mangal Inscript or Hindi Remington GAIL. The selected TYPiO course and Android’s active keyboard input method are related but separate settings.

Hindi inputAndroid controls the active input method. TYPiO maps supported QWERTY input for Hindi practice and accepts compatible Hindi Unicode output. Use the keyboard guide in the app to inspect what your device sends.
03

LESSONS OR PRACTICE

Choose how you want to type

Open a course lesson, select a passage or saved text, use a focused practice mode, take a test or challenge, or play a keyboard-based game. Practice can be untimed or set to 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 minutes.

Lesson rows show their status. Your speed goal helps mark a lesson as completed or goal not met; lessons remain available to select.

04

REVIEW YOUR SESSION

Use results as a guide

After a session, review measures such as speed, accuracy and mistakes, along with your lesson and session history. Results describe the recorded practice session; they are not a promise of a particular future speed.

05

OPTIONAL DATA BACKUP

Keep a copy if you choose

From Settings → Backup & export, create an encrypted JSON file and choose a device location, or connect Google Drive and enable backup. Drive uses Google’s authorization flow; TYPiO encrypts the backup with your passphrase before uploading its own Drive file.

Automatic Drive sync runs only when enabled, the app is open and internet is available. Keep your passphrase separately. TYPiO cannot recover it.
