01

THE APP

Practice that respects
your pace.

TYPiO turns a connected Android device and physical keyboard into a focused space to learn and practice typing.

Choose a guided lesson to work on particular keys, open a practice session with a passage or saved text, or take a typing test or challenge. After a session, review the measures TYPiO recorded and use them to decide what you want to practice next.

Typing practice and tests use a physical USB or Bluetooth keyboard. TYPiO does not show an on-screen typing keyboard during those sessions. Android controls the keyboard input method and layout used by your device.
