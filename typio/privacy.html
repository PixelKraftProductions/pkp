PRIVACY POLICY

Your data,
explained plainly.

TYPiO keeps app data on your device by default. This page explains what is stored and what happens when you choose a backup.

Publisher: Pixel Kraft ProductionsEffective: November 26, 2026Contact: mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com

AT A GLANCE

  • Core app works without a TYPiO account.
  • Learning records are stored on your device.
  • Drive backup is optional and uses Google authorization.
  • Backup content is encrypted before upload.
  • Your recovery passphrase is yours to keep.
Backup questions ↗

1. Who we are

TYPiO is an Android typing practice application published by Pixel Kraft Productions (“we”, “us” or “our”). This policy describes data handled by the current TYPiO app and its optional backup features.

2. Information stored on your device

TYPiO stores information in app-private storage to provide its features. This may include:

  • Preferences such as app language, typing course and layout, appearance, daily goal, speed display, sound and break preferences.
  • Session and learning records such as selected text, typed text, session type, language/layout, dates, duration, speed, accuracy, mistakes and lesson, test, challenge or game results.
  • Texts you explicitly save to your library.
  • An active-session recovery snapshot, which may include session settings and typed text so an interrupted session can be resumed.

Unsent editor drafts and Android keyboard settings are not part of the backup scope. See the app’s Backup & export screen for the current supported backup contents.

3. How local data is used

Local information helps TYPiO operate, remember your preferences, restore an interrupted session, calculate session results, show course progress, track lesson status and create a backup when you choose. Core app features do not require TYPiO registration.

4. Local storage and deletion

App data is stored in app-private storage on your Android device. You can delete saved texts or use the local-data controls in Settings → Backup & export to clear app data. Android’s app-data clearing or uninstalling TYPiO may remove local records. Files exported to a location you choose and a backup in Google Drive are managed separately and are not automatically removed by clearing TYPiO’s local data.

5. Encrypted file export

You may create an encrypted JSON backup and save it to a location selected through Android’s system file interface. The backup uses a passphrase you choose. Keep that passphrase separate from the backup file. TYPiO cannot recover a forgotten passphrase; without it, the encrypted backup cannot be restored.

6. Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is off unless you choose to connect a Google account and enable the feature. Google handles account authorization through its own sign-in and permission interface. TYPiO does not ask for or receive your Google password.

When you request a Drive backup, TYPiO encrypts the supported backup data on the device with your recovery passphrase before uploading its own backup file to the Google account you authorized. The file is stored in your visible Google Drive. Google processes and stores it under Google’s terms and privacy information. Automatic sync occurs only when you enable it, the app is open and an internet connection is available. You can disable automatic sync and manage or delete the Drive file through Google Drive.

Protect the passphrase. Anyone who obtains both the backup file and its passphrase may be able to access the backup contents.

7. Sharing, clipboard and network use

If you choose a share or clipboard action, the selected content is passed to the Android feature or other app you selected. That app handles the content under its own terms. Internet access is used for optional Google Drive backup and related account authorization.

8. Advertising and analytics

The current TYPiO app does not send typing results to Pixel Kraft Productions for advertising or analytics. Optional backup data is sent to Google Drive only when you enable backup and authorize access. Review the final app version’s Play Console Data safety disclosures for the complete set of SDK and platform behavior.

9. Children and audience

TYPiO is a general typing practice app. The intended audience and any age-related declarations are specified by the publisher in Google Play. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions concerning a child’s use.

10. Your choices and policy updates

You can choose whether to use Drive backup, turn off automatic sync, delete local app data, or remove your Drive backup through Google Drive. We may revise this policy when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the version published here.

11. Contact

For questions about TYPiO or this policy, contact mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com.
