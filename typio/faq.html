Practical details about keyboards, courses, progress and your backup choices.

GETTING STARTED

Do I need a physical keyboard?

Yes. TYPiO lessons, practice sessions and typing tests are designed for a connected USB or Bluetooth physical keyboard. The app does not provide an on-screen keyboard for typing sessions.

Can I use a mouse or touchscreen to move around?

Touch navigation is available for moving through the app. A mouse or trackpad may work depending on your Android device. Typing practice itself uses a physical keyboard.

Which typing courses are included?

TYPiO includes English QWERTY, Hindi Mangal Inscript and Hindi Remington GAIL Unicode lesson paths. Progress is tracked separately for each layout.

Can I use the app in portrait and landscape?

TYPiO adapts its layouts to supported screen orientations. Available orientation behavior can depend on your device and app version.

COURSES AND RESULTS

How does lesson status work?

Lesson status indicates whether a lesson is pending, completed, or whether its speed goal was not met. The speed goal is configurable in settings. Lessons remain available to select; you can revisit practice at any time.

What are WPM, KPM and accuracy?

WPM means words per minute; KPM means characters per minute. Accuracy summarizes correctly typed characters according to the session’s scoring. These are practice measurements, not a promise of an exam or employment result.

Does TYPiO save my session history?

Completed session results and learning progress are stored locally on your device. You can review available history in the Progress area and manage your local data in Settings.

How do I practice Hindi characters?

Choose a Hindi course in TYPiO and follow the lesson guidance. Android controls the active physical-keyboard input method. TYPiO maps supported QWERTY input to the selected Hindi layout, and compatible Hindi Unicode output from Android can pass through. The in-app keyboard guide can help you inspect keyboard output.

DATA AND BACKUP

Does TYPiO require an account?

No TYPiO registration is required for the core app. Google account authorization is needed only if you choose to connect Google Drive backup.

Does TYPiO see my Google password?

No. Google handles account authorization. TYPiO does not ask for or receive your Google password. If you opt in, the app uploads its own encrypted backup file to the Google account you authorize.

What is included in an encrypted backup?

Supported app preferences, course progress, session results and recovery data, and saved texts can be included. Unsaved editor drafts and Android keyboard settings are not included. Check the Backup & export screen for the current backup scope.

How does Google Drive backup work?

Connect a Google account through Google’s authorization prompt, set and keep your recovery passphrase, then choose whether to enable sync or start a backup. TYPiO encrypts the backup on the device before uploading its own visible backup file to your Drive. Automatic sync runs only when enabled, the app is open and internet is available.

Can TYPiO recover a forgotten backup passphrase?

No. Keep the passphrase in a safe place separate from the backup file. Without it, an encrypted backup cannot be restored by TYPiO.

What happens if I uninstall or clear app data?

Local app data may be removed. An exported file or Drive backup is separate and can be used to restore supported records. Restoring replaces the matching local app data after you review and confirm.
