About the adventure

Snakesss

A cozy, colorful take on the classic snake game.

Made by Pixel Kraft Productions · Last updated: November 8, 2026

Grow your little friend

Guide a friendly snake around the board, collect red apples to grow and earn 10 points each, and look for an optional golden apple worth 20 bonus points. Avoid the walls, stones, and your own tail as you work toward each level's apple goal.

Four worlds, one hundred trails

Explore Mosswood Meadow (Levels 1–25), Brookside Bend (26–50), Sunstone Canyon (51–75), and Moonlit Grove (76–100). Each world brings a different color identity and obstacle arrangements. Layouts are deterministic for each level, while hazards and apple goals gradually increase through the campaign.

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert. Each difficulty has its own Level 1 starting point, unlocks, completed levels, stars, scores, and best records. Changing difficulty does not erase the other difficulty's progress.

How stars are earned

  • Complete the level to earn at least one star.
  • Earn two stars by finishing within the move par, calculated as the level's apple goal × 5 + 12.
  • Collect the golden apple during the level to earn three stars.

Apple goals are based on the level and selected difficulty. The base goal rises by one every five levels, starting at five and reaching 21; Easy lowers the goal, while Hard and Expert raise it.

Play your way

  • Steer with gestures, the on-screen direction buttons, or both.
  • Choose a light or dark theme.
  • Adjust sound effects, vibration, and reduced motion in Settings.
  • Pause and continue an in-progress game.
  • Use the vibration test in Settings to check device feedback.
  • Play the core game offline; your progress and preferences are stored on your device.

What the game does not include

This version has no player accounts, online leaderboard, multiplayer, advertisements, or analytics. Android can check Google Play for eligible in-app updates on launch or resume and from Settings; update availability depends on the Play Store release and installation source.
