A small adventure, made for your pace

Snakesss brings a little extra heart to a classic. Explore Mosswood Meadow, Brookside Bend, Sunstone Canyon, and Moonlit Grove. Each world has its own look and obstacle patterns, and the trails add challenges as you advance.

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert. Each difficulty keeps its own unlocks, stars, scores, and completion records, so you can begin a separate adventure at Level 1 whenever you change the challenge.

Red apples grow your snake and add 10 points. Golden apples are optional bonuses worth 20 points. Earn one star for completing a level, two for completing it within its move par, or three by collecting its golden apple. Difficulty changes the apple goal and movement pace.

Swipe, use the on-screen arrows, or combine both controls. Customize light or dark mode, sound, vibration, and reduced motion in Settings. Pause and continue an in-progress run whenever you need a break.

Core gameplay works offline. Your progress and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not cloud-synced. Android may contact Google Play to check for an eligible app update.
