Privacy policy

App: Snakesss · Publisher: Pixel Kraft Productions
Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Snakesss mobile app. The game is designed to work without an account. Pixel Kraft Productions does not receive your gameplay data from the app. It applies to the current app behavior; the separate GitHub Pages website is described below.

Information stored on your device

To provide game features, Snakesss saves the following information in local app storage:

  • Campaign progress for each difficulty, completed levels, stars, and personal best scores.
  • Your current in-progress game, so you can continue it.
  • Preferences such as theme, difficulty, control mode, sound, vibration, and reduced motion.

The app does not upload or sync these records to Pixel Kraft Productions. They remain in app storage until you clear the app's data or remove the app. Operating-system backup and restore behavior may vary by platform and device. The app does not create an online backup of your progress.

Android update checks

On Android, Snakesss uses Google Play's in-app update service to check for an eligible update when the app starts or returns to the foreground, and when you choose “Check for updates” in Settings. If Google Play reports an eligible update, the app can offer the Google Play update flow. These checks require an internet connection and are not used to send your scores, campaign progress, or preferences to Pixel Kraft Productions.

Google Play and Google Play services may process device, app-installation, or account information to provide their update service. That processing is governed by Google's own terms and privacy notices. iOS builds do not use this in-app update feature.

Information we do not collect through the app

The current app has no player accounts, advertising, analytics, online leaderboard, or multiplayer service. It does not ask you to provide a name, email address, or location. The vibration setting controls local device feedback; it is not used to identify you.

Children and families

Snakesss does not request personal information from players. Because the app does not include account registration, advertising, or analytics, it does not use those features to profile players. Parents and guardians can manage app access and device permissions through the device's operating-system controls.

Security and retention

Game records are stored in the app's local storage. Keep your device protected. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove local game data. We do not maintain a server copy of these records.

Changes to this policy

If app behavior or this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Please review it from time to time.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer, Pixel Kraft Productions, at mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com.

About this website

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to operate and secure its hosting service under its own privacy terms. That website hosting is separate from the Snakesss app and its local game data.
