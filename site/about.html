Sharing a file should not ask you to learn a new system. WiFiles keeps the flow simple: find a device, choose what to share, review the details, then follow the transfer.

WiFiles is designed for Android, Mac, and Windows devices. Platform-specific connections adapt to each device, including the optional Mac Helper for one-tap browser launch.

Choose a nearby device, approve the session, and stay in control of what is sent and where received files are saved.
