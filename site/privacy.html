Policy.

This policy explains how WiFiles handles information when you use the Android app and its supported desktop companion experiences.

01 / AT A GLANCE

Local transfers, with your choice

WiFiles is designed to transfer files directly between devices you choose on the same local network. It does not require a WiFiles account or a WiFiles cloud relay for the documented transfer flows.

WiFiles handles the files and connection details needed to carry out the task you start. Your selected file is sent to the peer and destination you approve. Pixel Kraft Productions does not receive the file through a WiFiles server.

Who this covers

This policy applies to WiFiles for Android and the compatible browser, macOS Helper, and Windows PC flows described in the WiFiles product documentation. Available features can vary by platform.

02 / INFORMATION HANDLED

What WiFiles uses to work

Files you select

When you choose files to send, WiFiles reads those selections to prepare and carry out the transfer. A receiving session can write files only to the destination you select or approve. File contents are transferred to the peer device you choose; they are not uploaded to Pixel Kraft Productions.

Device and session details

Device names, local network addresses, pairing or QR details, temporary session credentials, selected-file names and sizes in a transfer manifest, and transfer progress may be processed on the participating devices to discover, identify, authorize, and complete a transfer. File paths, QR payloads, pairing credentials, and session tokens are not written to diagnostic logs or sent to Pixel Kraft Productions. Session credentials are temporary and expire or are revoked when the session ends. Pairing information for a trusted device remains on the devices until you revoke that trust or clear the app’s local data.

Transfer history and preferences

WiFiles may keep a local transfer record, such as file names, sizes, dates, destination device, and result, so you can review recent activity. App preferences, including theme and language, are stored locally. These records are not sent to Pixel Kraft Productions.

Diagnostics

Connection troubleshooting runs on the device when you request it. WiFiles does not send diagnostic reports, analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports to Pixel Kraft Productions. The WiFiles v1 release includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Diagnostics run locally only after you request them; diagnostic reports are not uploaded.

03 / SHARING & STORAGE

Where information goes

  • Your chosen peer: files and the transfer information needed to receive them go to the device you connect to and approve.
  • Pixel Kraft Productions: WiFiles does not operate a cloud account or relay for these local transfer flows, and does not receive file contents, device discovery results, or transfer history through WiFiles servers.
  • People on your local network: nearby devices and the network operator may be able to observe network activity. Use a network you trust; WiFiles cannot make a public or shared network private.
  • Platform providers: Android, macOS, browsers, and app stores process information under their own terms when you use their operating systems, browser, app distribution, or system file pickers.

The Mac Helper may make a user-started request to the configured signed-update service. That request can disclose the Helper version, the network address needed to serve the request, and standard request metadata to the configured update host. It does not include files, file names, transfer manifests, session credentials, or device identity. Android app updates are provided through the app store from which you installed WiFiles.

Files saved by the receiving device remain in the destination chosen there until someone with access to that device deletes them. Deleting a WiFiles history entry does not delete a file already saved on either device.

04 / RETENTION & DELETION

You control local records

  • Temporary sessions: connection credentials are limited to the active session and expire or are revoked when it is stopped, expires, or is revoked.
  • Trusted devices: pairing details stay on the participating devices until you remove the trusted device, clear WiFiles app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Transfer history: records stay on the device where WiFiles created them until you delete them in the app, clear app data, or uninstall it. Clearing history does not erase transferred copies.
  • Received and sent files: files remain wherever you or the receiving device saved them. Delete those copies using the file controls on the relevant device.
  • Support messages: if you email us, Pixel Kraft Productions uses the information in your message to respond and handle the request. We delete the message within 90 days after the request is resolved unless it must be retained for a legal obligation or to resolve a dispute.

WiFiles does not provide user accounts. There is no WiFiles account or central transfer archive to delete. For privacy questions or a request about information you emailed us, contact mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com.

05 / DEVICE PERMISSIONS

Access is tied to the task

  • File picker or selected-folder access: lets you choose the files to send or the destination where received files are saved. WiFiles uses the files and folders you select for those actions.
  • Local network access: lets WiFiles find and connect to nearby peers and run a transfer session. WiFiles does not use this permission to determine your physical location or send your location to Pixel Kraft Productions.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. WiFiles does not use the camera for background capture.
  • Notifications: where supported and enabled, show active transfer or receive-session status and controls.
  • macOS folder access: the Mac Helper uses folders selected by the Mac owner for an enabled Drop Zone. It does not grant a phone general access to the Mac’s files.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

06 / SECURITY

Connection safeguards

WiFiles uses explicit device pairing or session approval, short-lived session credentials, an authenticated and encrypted local transfer session, scoped file and folder access, and user-controlled stop and revoke actions. Keep WiFiles and your device software updated, approve only devices you recognize, and avoid transferring sensitive files over networks you do not trust. No local-network transfer can guarantee that a network itself is private.

07 / WEBSITE

This website

The WiFiles website is a static information site. It does not provide an account, file upload, or contact form, and it does not add advertising or analytics trackers. Choosing a contact link opens your email application; information you send is handled as a support message described above. The site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information to serve the site under its own privacy terms.

The version available at this URL is the current policy.
