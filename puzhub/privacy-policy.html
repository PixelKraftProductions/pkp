Privacy policy

This policy describes how PuzHub: Daily Brain Puzzles handles information when you use the Android app and this website.

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Applies to: PuzHub Android app and this website

PuzHub is designed as an offline, single-player puzzle app. In the current release reviewed for this policy, game progress, settings, records, and an optional selected picture are stored locally on your device. No PuzHub account or PuzHub-operated game server is used.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

PuzHub: Daily Brain Puzzles is published by Pixel Kraft Productions (“we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions or requests, email mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com. The app is distributed for Android through Google Play. Google independently processes information as described in its own notices and policies when you use Google Play or Android services.

2. Information the app stores

The current app does not ask you to create an account and does not request your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or microphone recordings. It saves the following information in app-private storage on your device so features can work:

  • Game state and progress, including current or unlocked levels and locally saved sessions.
  • Local game records and results (the app keeps a limited recent history, up to 100 records).
  • Your preferences, such as theme, sound, vibration, and game options.
  • If you choose a picture for Picture Slide, a resized copy of that picture and the associated local puzzle state.

This storage is not presented as encrypted. It is held in the app’s private area using Android app storage. Other apps ordinarily cannot access that area without elevated device access. Device backup behavior may depend on Android and distribution settings; the current Android manifest disables app backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes its app-private data.

3. Optional pictures and Android permissions

Picture Slide can be played with built-in artwork or an image you choose. If you choose a picture, Android’s system picker lets you select it. The app uses the selected image only to prepare and display the puzzle on your device. The app may resize/crop it and keep the current selection plus a small number of recent selections to support gameplay and transitions. It does not upload the picture to Pixel Kraft Productions.

You can switch back to built-in artwork or remove the app’s stored image by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. You can decline to select a picture and continue using all other games. The app also uses vibration feedback when enabled; you can turn it off in Settings. It does not need camera capture, microphone, contacts, or location for the described gameplay.

Android may display permissions or system UI associated with selecting media or using vibration. The exact permission behavior can vary by Android version and the installed app build. You control whether to grant access through Android settings and the system picker.

4. Sharing, analytics, advertising, and network access

In the current release reviewed for this policy, the app source/configuration has no active advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash reporting service, in-app purchase service, sign-in provider, or PuzHub cloud synchronization. We do not sell personal information. Game progress and selected puzzle pictures are not sent to our servers because the app does not operate a game-data server.

The Android app declares internet capability. That declaration alone does not mean your game data is transmitted. Google Play, Android, the device maker, or other platform services may independently process technical information under their own terms. This public website is hosted through GitHub Pages; when you visit it, GitHub may receive routine request and security logs (such as IP address, browser, and requested page) under GitHub’s policies. We do not add tracking pixels, advertising, analytics, cookies, or third-party web fonts to this site.

If a future app version adds a third-party SDK or online feature, we will update this policy and the app’s disclosures before or when that change is released, and the relevant store disclosures must also be updated.

5. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. You can clear all app data from Android Settings → Apps → PuzHub → Storage → Clear storage (menu names can vary by device). To remove a selected picture while keeping the app, switch Picture Slide back to a built-in scene where that option is available. Uninstalling PuzHub removes its app-private files.

We do not hold a copy of local game data and therefore cannot retrieve or erase it remotely. If you email us, we will use your message and email address only to answer your request and keep it only as long as reasonably needed for that correspondence or to meet legal obligations.

6. Children’s privacy

PuzHub is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or online profile. The app’s gameplay is local and does not include chat, public user profiles, or user-to-user messaging. Parents and guardians can contact us at mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com with a privacy concern. App distribution, age ratings, and family eligibility are determined separately through Google Play and must match the actual release.

7. Security

We use Android’s app-private storage for local game data and do not operate a server containing that data. No method of device storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device operating system updated and use its screen lock and security controls. Do not use a picture you are not comfortable storing locally on your device.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can use the games without supplying contact details or selecting a photo. You can change theme, sound, and vibration settings in the app, decline the system photo picker, clear app storage, or uninstall. To ask a question or make a request about information you sent to us by email, contact mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request and may retain limited correspondence where required by law.

9. International use

Local gameplay data stays on your device unless you choose to share it outside the app. Email support and hosting of this policy may involve service providers operating in different countries. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version. For a material change, we will use an appropriate notice in the app or on this page where practical.
