About PuzHub

PuzHub: Daily Brain Puzzles is a collection of five casual logic and matching games for Android, made by Pixel Kraft Productions.

123

A little play, at your pace.

Choose a short round or settle into a longer puzzle. Number Slide and Picture Slide use generated shuffles. Memory Match deals a fresh card layout. Block Puzzle creates new piece trays. Sudoku creates varied grids from a valid base puzzle.

Game level progress, active session, preferences, and up to 100 recent results are stored locally using the app’s private storage. The games do not need an account or an internet connection to play.

Current Android app

What is included

  • Five game modes with separate level progress.
  • Light and Dark appearance options.
  • Sound effects and ambient game audio controls.
  • Optional haptic feedback control.
  • Six vector scenes and an optional user-selected photo for Picture Slide.
  • On-device session recovery and personal records.

The current release does not include a PuzHub account, cloud sync, advertising provider, or analytics SDK. Details are in the privacy policy.

Progression

What happens after a level?

Completing a level advances that game’s saved level number. New rounds are generated when started; PuzHub is not a finite, numbered pack of hand-authored boards. Board size or pair count can grow with progression in the games that use those values. Block Puzzle is an ongoing Classic run that ends when the current board has no legal placement.

Completed results include the game’s relevant time, moves, turns, or score. PuzHub keeps the most recent 100 records on the device.

Personal settings

Set the feel of your session

Light and Dark

Switch the app’s visual theme in Settings. Your theme choice is stored locally.

Sound and haptics

Toggle audio feedback and vibration responses separately. They can be disabled without affecting puzzle rules.

Your picture

Choose a photo through the gallery picker, crop it for a square board, and use it offline. The imported copy remains in app-private storage.
