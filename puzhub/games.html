Rules, without the mystery

Meet the games

Every mode has a clear goal and its own way to play. Here is what each one does, what its controls mean, and how its levels change.

8

Number Slide

Arrange the numbered tiles in order by sliding a tile into the adjacent empty space. The board is shuffled through valid moves, so its starting position remains solvable.

  • Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard to set a starting board size: 3 × 3, 4 × 4, or 5 × 5.
  • Move one tile at a time into the empty square. Each slide counts as a move.
  • Finish when the numbers read in order and the empty space is last.
  • Later levels increase the challenge; the board grows to a maximum of 5 × 5.

There is no countdown deadline. A local timer and move count help you compare your own completed runs.

◈

Picture Slide

Reassemble a square picture by sliding one tile at a time into the open space, using the same sliding rule as Number Slide.

  • Choose one of six bundled vector scenes: Quiet Coast, Alpine Air, Desert Glow, Moonlit Bay, Forest Canopy, or Blossom Valley.
  • Or choose a photo from your device, crop it to the square puzzle, and play with it offline.
  • Choose Easy (3 × 3), Medium (4 × 4), or Hard (5 × 5). Tile numbers can also be shown as an aid.
  • Complete the picture when every tile returns to its original position.

Your selected photo is copied into PuzHub’s private app storage. PuzHub does not upload it to a developer server.

✿

Memory Match

Turn over two face-down cards at a time. If their pictures match, the pair stays visible. If not, remember them and try another pair.

  • Choose Animals, Shapes, or Objects.
  • Easy starts with 6 pairs on a 3 × 4 layout; Medium with 8 pairs on 4 × 4; Hard with 10 pairs on 4 × 5.
  • Later levels can add pairs, up to 12.
  • Complete the round by finding every pair. The game records time and turns locally.
▦

Block Puzzle

Place pieces on an 8 × 8 board. Fill an entire row or column to clear that line and open space for more pieces.

  1. Select one of the three offered pieces.
  2. Tap a legal empty starting cell to place it.
  3. After using all three pieces, PuzHub creates another tray.
  4. Keep placing pieces and clearing lines. The run ends when no current piece fits.

Classic mode has no time limit. The score and personal best are saved on the device.

9

Sudoku

Fill the 9 × 9 grid so each row, column, and 3 × 3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 once.

  • Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert.
  • Select an empty cell and tap a digit to enter it.
  • Use Notes to pencil in candidates, Erase to remove an entry, and Undo to revert a move.
  • Conflict feedback helps identify repeated digits in a row, column, or box.

Each level creates a varied Sudoku board from a valid puzzle pattern. The timer is local to the session; there is no online leaderboard or account.

Levels and saved progress

New rounds are generated as you play

Level progress is tracked separately for each game. When a puzzle is completed, that game advances to the next level. PuzHub generates the next starting board or card arrangement in the app; you do not need to download a level pack.

Progress, sessions, settings, selected picture details, and recent results stay in local app storage. Removing app data or uninstalling PuzHub removes those local records.
